# Grammar Path

Suggested sequence:

1. subject pronouns, articles, gender, number
2. present tense and high-frequency irregular verbs
3. questions, negation, adjectives, prepositions
4. passé composé and imperfect
5. future forms and object pronouns
6. relative pronouns and complex sentences
7. conditional and subjunctive in high-value contexts
8. exam-focused accuracy and editing

Reorder when diagnostic evidence shows a prerequisite gap.
