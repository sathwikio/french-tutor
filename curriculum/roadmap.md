# Curriculum Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Baseline

Diagnose listening, speaking, reading, writing, grammar, and vocabulary. Set an evidence-based starting level.

## Phase 1 — Foundation

Build high-frequency vocabulary, sound-letter mapping, sentence structure, present tense, articles, agreement, negation, and essential communication.

## Phase 2 — Independent communication

Develop past and future narration, connected speech and writing, broader vocabulary, reading strategies, and routine listening practice.

## Phase 3 — TEF development

Introduce task formats, timed production, comprehension strategies, scoring criteria, and targeted remediation.

## Phase 4 — Exam readiness

Use timed sections, full simulations, score tracking, error analysis, and tapering review.

Progress by demonstrated performance, not elapsed time.
