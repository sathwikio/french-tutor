# Session Progression

## Default 30-minute session

- 3 min: retrieval warm-up
- 7 min: focused instruction
- 10 min: guided practice
- 7 min: independent production
- 3 min: feedback and handoff

## Longer session

Add listening or reading input, then an integrated speaking or writing response.

## Progression rule

Advance when the learner succeeds unaided across varied examples. Otherwise change the explanation, reduce complexity, and retest.
